# Stop 529 contributions at college start

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-07

529 contributions stop at college start. Derive that date from birth dates plus age 18. Age 18 is the stated default. Qualified withdrawals can start then. The new contribution amount is zero.

This timeline is the spending-pack rule that the kids and dependents care-cost entry points at. It does not calculate a household's 529 balance.

Sources: [IRS Publication 970: tax benefits for education](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p970).
