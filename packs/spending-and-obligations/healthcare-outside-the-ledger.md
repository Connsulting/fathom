# Keep payroll and business premiums outside household spending

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-07

Premiums paid through payroll, or paid by a business account, are outside the household ledger. They never appear in the household healthcare class. The healthcare class is only premiums and medical costs that post as household transactions.

Health & Wellness can include a gym membership. A saved spending definition can move that category out of healthcare.

Sources: [IRS Publication 502: medical and dental expenses](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p502).
