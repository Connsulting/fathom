# Project spending after obligations change

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-07

An obligation schedule states the monthly amount now, the date the amount ends or changes, and the new amount. A spending projection applies those changes to a spending base and does not inflate the result.

Mortgage: the payment drops at payoff to the escrow amount, which continues as housing. Childcare: the amount changes at school entry (age 5) and ends at age 13. 529 contributions stop at college start (age 18) and the new amount is zero.

A missing date or amount stays a gap. Do not invent a payoff, a birth date, or a dollar figure in prose.

Sources: [IRS Publication 936: home mortgage interest](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p936), [IRS Publication 503: child and dependent care](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p503), [IRS Publication 970: tax benefits for education](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p970).
