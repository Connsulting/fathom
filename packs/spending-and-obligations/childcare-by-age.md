# Change childcare cost at school entry and age 13

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-07

Childcare changes with the child's age. Use dependent birth dates when they exist. Full-time care runs to school entry at age 5, by September 1 of that year. That September 1 date is the stated default. The owner can override it. After-school care runs to age 13, the dependent care credit's age limit for a child who is not disabled.

If birth dates are missing, use dependent ages with year precision. If those are missing, use an owner-stated childcare change date.

The new amount at school entry is the stated school-age monthly cost. At age 13 the pack default is zero: care costs stop. An owner assumption can replace either amount. If neither the fact nor an assumption supplies the new amount, leave it unavailable and do not apply a change.

Sources: [IRS Publication 503: child and dependent care](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p503).
