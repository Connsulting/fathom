# Schedule Virginia estimated payments independently

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-05

For taxable years beginning in 2026, Virginia generally requires estimated payments or additional withholding when expected Virginia tax after withholding and allowable credits exceeds $1,000, subject to the instructions' filing exceptions. Do not reuse the older $150 threshold from prior-year material. A household with wage withholding and consulting or partnership income must consider their combined state result.

The 2026 Form 760ES calendar-year installment dates are May 1, 2026, June 15, 2026, September 15, 2026, and January 15, 2027. Virginia's first installment date differs from the usual federal schedule. Special rules apply when the obligation first arises later in the year, and for qualifying farming, fishing and merchant-seaman income.

Recommendation: retain confirmation of each payment and its designated year, and reconcile any prior-year overpayment applied forward. Recompute remaining installments after income changes instead of assuming that a year-end balance payment cures every earlier underpayment. Use the Virginia worksheet and current underpayment rules separately from the federal calculation; a federal safe harbor is not proof of adequate state payment.

Sources: [Virginia Tax 2026 Form 760ES and instructions](https://www.tax.virginia.gov/sites/default/files/taxforms/individual-income-tax/2026/760es-2026.pdf).
