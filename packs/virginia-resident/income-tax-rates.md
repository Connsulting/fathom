# Apply Virginia marginal income tax rates

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-05

For 2026, Virginia taxes the first $3,000 of Virginia taxable income at 2%, the portion over $3,000 through $5,000 at 3%, the portion over $5,000 through $17,000 at 5%, and the portion above $17,000 at 5.75%. These are marginal bands; reaching the top band does not tax every dollar at 5.75%.

The bands apply to Virginia taxable income after the applicable additions, subtractions, exemptions and deductions, not directly to federal AGI, business receipts or account balances. Apply the filing and spouse-allocation rules for the actual return before calculating a household result.

Recommendation: project the state return separately when salary, pass-through income, retirement contributions or charitable giving changes. Reconcile state withholding and estimates against that result using Form 760ES. The ordinary state payment safe harbor uses 90% of current-year liability or 100% of prior-year liability, whichever is less, with timely installments; do not import the federal 110% high-income tier.

Sources: [Code of Virginia 58.1-320: marginal income tax rates](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title58.1/section58.1-320/), [Virginia Tax 2026 Form 760ES and instructions](https://www.tax.virginia.gov/sites/default/files/taxforms/individual-income-tax/2026/760es-2026.pdf).
