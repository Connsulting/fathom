# Review other-state credits and wage reciprocity

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-05

A Virginia resident generally reports income from all sources. When another state also taxes qualifying income, Virginia may allow a credit for income tax paid there. The credit is subject to its own income categories, calculation and documentation; it is not a refund of every dollar withheld by an out-of-state employer.

Virginia identifies qualifying earned income, business income and certain capital-asset gains for this credit. A resident claiming credits for multiple states computes them separately and retains the other states' returns and supporting schedules. Part-year residency and dual-residency situations require careful allocation rather than a blanket credit for an entire year's tax.

Reciprocity can mean the other state should refund wage withholding instead of Virginia granting a credit. The applicable state and work facts matter, and wage reciprocity does not automatically cover partnership or consulting business income. Recommendation: resolve residency, sourcing and reciprocity before projecting a credit. Coordinate the result with W-2 payroll elections and the business packs so the same income and payment are not omitted or counted twice.

Sources: [Virginia Tax credit for taxes paid to another state](https://www.tax.virginia.gov/credit-for-taxes-paid-to-another-state), [Virginia Tax residency status](https://www.tax.virginia.gov/residency-status).
