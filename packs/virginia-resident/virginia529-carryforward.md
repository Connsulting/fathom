# Track Virginia529 carryforwards and the age-70 exception

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-05

Eligible contributions above the ordinary $4,000 per-account annual Virginia deduction limit can carry forward until deducted. Keep each account's contribution basis, deductions already taken and unused carryforward separately. The carryforward belongs to the account's tax history; it does not increase merely because an account statement shows investment growth.

A purchaser or contributor who has attained age 70 is exempt from the ordinary annual cap and may deduct eligible contributed amounts not previously deducted. A transfer of ownership carries the account's tax attributes, including carryforward and recapture exposure, to the transferee under the statute. The age rule does not permit deducting the same contribution twice.

The program description notes Virginia Tax rulings requiring eligible deductions and carryovers to be claimed in successive eligible years. Recommendation: reconcile prior returns before treating every previously unclaimed contribution as freely deferrable. An ownership transfer, move or age milestone calls for a state review of who can use the remaining attributes. Maintain the records even after contributions stop because a later withdrawal can still affect recapture.

Sources: [Code of Virginia 58.1-322.03: deductions](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title58.1/section58.1-322.03/), [Invest529 Program Description, October 1, 2026](https://www.invest529.com/uploads/files/va529_invest_program_description.pdf).
