# Separate qualified withdrawals from Virginia recapture

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-05

Virginia can recapture previously claimed education-account deductions when a distribution or refund is made for a nonqualifying reason. The statute specifies exceptions involving qualified higher education expenses and the beneficiary's death, disability or scholarship. Keep prior state deductions, the withdrawal purpose and supporting expenses together; federal earnings tax and Virginia deduction recapture are different questions.

The current Invest529 program description includes expanded 2026 K-12 uses and a $20,000 annual K-12 limit per beneficiary, aggregated across accounts. That does not make every household education expense qualified. Confirm the specific expense category, date and applicable federal and state rules before withdrawing.

Open question: the October 1, 2026 program description states that Virginia Tax has not yet commented on state treatment of transfers to Virginia ABLEnow or 529-to-Roth IRA rollovers. Do not promise exemption from Virginia recapture on those transactions. An outbound rollover to another state's program can also require recapture under the program rules. Recommendation: obtain transaction-specific state guidance before relying on favorable treatment and preserve the account's complete deduction history.

Sources: [Code of Virginia 58.1-322.03: deductions](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title58.1/section58.1-322.03/), [Invest529 Program Description, October 1, 2026](https://www.invest529.com/uploads/files/va529_invest_program_description.pdf).
