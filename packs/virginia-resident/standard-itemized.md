# Coordinate federal and Virginia deduction choices

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-05

For tax year 2026, Virginia's standard deduction is $8,750 for single individuals and $17,500 for married persons filing jointly. The married separate amount is one-half of the married amount. The statute conditions the standard deduction on not itemizing on the federal return, so evaluate the federal and state results together when choosing the federal deduction method.

Virginia itemized deductions begin with allowable federal itemized deductions but require state adjustments, including removing deducted state and local income taxes. Virginia also retains an overall itemized-deduction limitation described in the state's conformity guidance. Do not apply the federal taxable-income result directly as Virginia taxable income.

Published law provides a 2027 Virginia standard deduction of $9,200 single and $18,400 married; those amounts do not replace the 2026 figures. Recommendation: compare charitable bunching and other deductions across both returns with their correct tax years. A large federal deduction or a federal nonitemizer benefit does not establish an equal Virginia deduction or identical marginal savings.

Sources: [Code of Virginia 58.1-322.03: deductions](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title58.1/section58.1-322.03/), [Virginia Tax Bulletin 26-1](https://www.tax.virginia.gov/sites/default/files/inline-files/tb-26-1-date-of-irc-conformity-advanced.pdf).
