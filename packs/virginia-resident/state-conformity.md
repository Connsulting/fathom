# Apply Virginia conformity and adjustments separately

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-05

Virginia's current conformity date is December 31, 2025, with a limited rule for later federal amendments extending provisions to which Virginia conforms or previously conformed. Fixed-date conformity does not mean every federal rule effective in 2026 is excluded: legislation already enacted by the conformity date can have later effective dates. It also does not mean every federal deduction is available unchanged.

Tax Bulletin 26-1 describes continued differences for bonus depreciation and certain business expensing and research provisions. Virginia conforms to the federal change to the business-interest deduction limitation under IRC 163(j). For taxable years 2025 and thereafter, Virginia reduces its own subtraction from 50% to 20% of business interest disallowed on the federal return. It also explains that Virginia retains its own overall itemized-deduction limitation rather than adopting all federal changes. Maintain separate Virginia tax attributes when a business deduction's federal and state timing differs.

Recommendation: reconcile the federal return to Virginia additions, subtractions and deductions using current state instructions. Check both the enactment date and the particular exception before carrying a new federal rule into a state projection. For education accounts, examine the state deduction and recapture provisions directly; federal permission for a withdrawal or rollover alone is insufficient evidence of Virginia treatment.

Sources: [Code of Virginia 58.1-301: conformity](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title58.1/section58.1-301/), [Virginia Tax Bulletin 26-1](https://www.tax.virginia.gov/sites/default/files/inline-files/tb-26-1-date-of-irc-conformity-advanced.pdf).
