# Claim the Virginia529 deduction by qualifying account

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-05

Virginia allows a deduction for contributions to qualifying Commonwealth Savers education accounts, including Invest529. For an owner under age 70, the ordinary deduction limit is $4,000 per account per tax year, limited by the eligible contribution and applicable carryforward. It is a deduction from Virginia taxable income, rather than a dollar-for-dollar tax credit or a federal deduction.

The relevant purchaser or contributor is the person recorded by the plan as of December 31. A gift deposited by a relative does not automatically give that relative the account owner's Virginia deduction. Confirm the ownership record and retain the contribution history. The same amount cannot qualify here if it was deducted on the purchaser's or contributor's federal return.

Recommendation: review the actual account structure, ownership, prior deductions and funding timing before year-end. Do not confuse the state per-account deduction ceiling with the beneficiary-wide limits on particular withdrawals. Coordinate contributions with the kids-dependents pack and household cash reserves; tax eligibility does not itself establish that the contribution is affordable.

Sources: [Code of Virginia 58.1-322.03: deductions](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title58.1/section58.1-322.03/), [Invest529 Program Description, October 1, 2026](https://www.invest529.com/uploads/files/va529_invest_program_description.pdf).
