# QBI, owner wages and the 2026 thresholds

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-05

## Rule

Eligible pass-through business income can support a deduction of up to 20%, subject to taxable income and other limits. Reasonable S corporation compensation is excluded from the recipient's QBI. Wages and other properly allocable business deductions can reduce business QBI. A service business can face the specified service trade or business rules as household taxable income rises. [IRS qualified business income deduction](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/qualified-business-income-deduction) [IRS Form 8995 instructions, enduring QBI definitions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8995)

## 2026 thresholds

The taxable income threshold before the QBI deduction is $403,500 for married filing jointly, $201,775 for married filing separately and $201,750 for other returns. The corresponding phase-in ranges end at $553,500, $276,775 and $276,750. These are taxable income thresholds, not AGI thresholds and not automatic eligibility cutoffs for every business. [IRS Revenue Procedure 2025-32, 2026 inflation adjustments](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-25-32.pdf)

## Trap and interaction

Beginning in 2026, a $400 minimum deduction may apply with at least $1,000 of active qualified business income, subject to eligibility. Review the applicable rules rather than replacing every calculation with 20% of a K-1. Coordinate wages, employer retirement contributions, shareholder health deductions and other household income before comparing outcomes. The pack supplies statutory figures, not a personalized deduction estimate.
