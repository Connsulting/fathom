# Reasonable compensation before distributions

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-05

## Rule

An owner who performs services for an S corporation must receive reasonable compensation for those services before receiving nonwage distributions. The IRS can reclassify distributions as wages subject to employment taxes. There is no universal salary percentage or fixed salary safe harbor in this guidance. [IRS S corporation compensation and medical insurance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/s-corporation-compensation-and-medical-insurance-issues)

## Review

Document duties, time spent, training, responsibility, comparable pay and whether revenue comes from the owner's services, other employees or business capital. Administrative work performed by the owner also counts. Review compensation when the work or revenue mix changes, rather than setting wages solely to reach a desired retirement contribution. [IRS S corporation compensation and medical insurance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/s-corporation-compensation-and-medical-insurance-issues)

## Interaction

Compensation affects payroll taxes, retirement plan compensation and business profit used for QBI. A retirement limit or QBI target does not establish reasonable pay. This pack supplies review questions; the salary decision needs the business facts and professional judgment. [IRS retirement plan contributions for S corporations](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/retirement-plan-faqs-regarding-contributions-s-corporation) [IRS qualified business income deduction](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/qualified-business-income-deduction)
