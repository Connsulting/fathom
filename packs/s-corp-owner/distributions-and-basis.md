# Distributions, shareholder loans and basis

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-05

## Rule

A nondividend distribution is generally tax free only up to stock basis. Debt basis does not make a distribution tax free. Income can be taxable to the shareholder without a matching cash distribution, and a Schedule K-1's distribution amount does not establish the taxable amount. The shareholder tracks basis annually. [IRS S corporation stock and debt basis](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/s-corporation-stock-and-debt-basis)

## Review

Reconcile contributions, income, distributions, nondeductible expenses and losses in the required order. Loss deductions first face the stock and debt basis limits and can then face at-risk and passive activity limits. Form 7203 supports the basis computation. Debt basis generally requires a direct shareholder loan; repayment of a loan whose basis was reduced by deducted losses can itself be taxable. [IRS S corporation stock and debt basis](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/s-corporation-stock-and-debt-basis)

## Trap and interaction

Available cash and book equity do not establish tax basis. Former C corporation earnings can create dividend treatment, so do not apply the ordinary nondividend rule automatically. Keep payroll compensation separate from owner withdrawals and reconcile the basis effect before a planned distribution.
