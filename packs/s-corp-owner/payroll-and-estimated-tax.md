# Payroll deposits and household estimated tax

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-05

## Rule

The corporation handles wage withholding and employer payroll taxes separately from the owner's tax on pass-through profit. For 2026, Social Security is 6.2% each for employer and employee on wages up to $184,500. Medicare is 1.45% each with no wage cap. Employers also withhold 0.9% Additional Medicare Tax on wages they pay above $200,000, regardless of filing status; final household liability can differ. [IRS Publication 15, 2026 employer tax guide](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15)

## Deadlines

Payroll deposits follow the employer's applicable deposit schedule, which differs from return filing dates. For a calendar-year individual, ordinary 2026 estimated installments are due April 15, June 15 and September 15, 2026, and January 15, 2027. A filing extension does not turn quarterly payments into a year-end obligation. [IRS Publication 15, 2026 employer tax guide](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15) [IRS Publication 505, 2026 estimated tax](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p505)

## Trap and interaction

The general federal required annual payment compares 90% of current-year tax with 100% of prior-year tax, increased to 110% when prior-year AGI exceeds $150,000, or $75,000 for married filing separately. The prior return must cover a full year. Apply the detailed rules, credits and exceptions before computing installments; uneven income may justify annualization. Include wages, pass-through profit, spouse income and Roth conversions in the same household projection. [IRS Publication 505, 2026 estimated tax](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p505)
