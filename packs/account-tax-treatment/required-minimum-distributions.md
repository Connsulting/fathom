# Required minimum distributions

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-07

The start age depends on the birth date. Born before July 1, 1949, the start age was 70 and a half, already past for anyone that age. Born July 1, 1949 through 1950, the start age is 72. For 1951 through 1959 it is 73. For 1960 and later it is 75. A Roth IRA and a Roth 401(k) have no required minimum distribution for the original owner. This entry states the ages and birth years. It does not compute a distribution amount.

Sources: [IRS Publication 590-B: distributions from individual retirement arrangements](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p590b).
