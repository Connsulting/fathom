# Required minimum distributions

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-07

The start age depends on the birth year. It is 72 for 1950 and earlier, for a birth date after June 30, 1949. It is 73 for 1951 to 1959. It is 75 for 1960 and later. A Roth IRA and a Roth 401(k) have no required minimum distribution for the original owner. This entry states the ages and birth years. It does not compute a distribution amount.

Sources: [IRS Publication 590-B: distributions from individual retirement arrangements](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p590b).
