# HSA and 529 withdrawals

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-07

An HSA distribution for qualified medical costs is tax-free, including past costs when the owner has the receipts. Any other use is ordinary income plus a 20 percent additional tax before age 65. At 65, nonmedical use is ordinary income without that additional tax. An HSA has no required minimum distribution.

A 529 distribution for qualified education is tax-free. Earnings on other use are taxed, plus 10 percent. A 529 has no required minimum distribution. Contributions are after-tax, with a state deduction in some states.

Sources: [IRS Publication 969: health savings accounts](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p969). [IRS Publication 970: tax benefits for education](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p970).
