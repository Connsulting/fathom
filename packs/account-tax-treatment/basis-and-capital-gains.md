# Basis and capital gains

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-07

A taxable brokerage account is funded with after-tax money. Dividends and realized gains are taxed in the year they occur. On a sale, gain over basis is capital gain, long-term after one year, and basis returns untaxed. Cash is after-tax. Interest is taxed yearly. A cash withdrawal is untaxed. Either can be used any time. Neither has a required minimum distribution.

Sources: [IRS Publication 550: investment income and expenses](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p550).
