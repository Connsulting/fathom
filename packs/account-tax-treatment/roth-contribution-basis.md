# Roth contribution basis

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-07

Roth IRA contributions can come out any time, tax and penalty free, up to the contribution basis. Earnings need both age 59 and a half and the five-year rule. The five-year clock starts with the first Roth contribution year. A Roth 401(k) qualified withdrawal also needs age 59 and a half and the five-year rule. Without a recorded first contribution year, the qualified date stays unavailable.

Sources: [IRS Publication 590-B: distributions from individual retirement arrangements](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p590b).
