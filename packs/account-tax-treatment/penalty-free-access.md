# Penalty-free access

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-07

The usual penalty-free age for a retirement distribution is 59 and a half. Employer plans have an extra exception called the rule of 55: separation from that employer in or after the year of turning 55. The employer plan types are 401(k), Roth 401(k), solo 401(k), 403(b) and 457(b). The rule of 55 does not apply to IRAs. Substantially equal periodic payments are a separate exception. This entry states the ages. It does not compute a household date.

Sources: [Internal Revenue Code section 72](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section72&num=0&edition=prelim). [IRS Publication 575: pension and annuity income](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p575).
