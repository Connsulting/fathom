# Include the 2026 SALT cap and phase-down in itemization

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-05

For 2026, the federal itemized deduction for covered personal state and local taxes is capped at $40,400, or $20,200 for married filing separately. Covered taxes include eligible real and personal property taxes plus either state and local income taxes or general sales taxes. The cap is a ceiling on eligible taxes paid, not an additional deduction simply for reaching that amount.

The 2026 cap is reduced by 30% of the amount by which modified adjusted gross income exceeds $505,000, or $252,500 for married filing separately. The reduced cap cannot fall below $10,000, or $5,000 for married filing separately. For this provision, MAGI is AGI increased by income excluded under sections 911, 931 and 933. Use these year-specific values rather than the old flat cap or an unmodified AGI estimate.

Recommendation: apply this cap and phase-down before comparing total itemized deductions with the standard deduction or evaluating charitable bunching. A large state tax bill does not guarantee a matching federal deduction. The separate section 68 itemized-deduction limit can reduce deductions further; Virginia also adjusts deducted state and local income taxes on its own return.

Sources: [IRS Publication 505 (2026): SALT cap and MAGI thresholds](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p505), [Internal Revenue Code 164(b)(6)-(7): SALT limit and phase-down](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t26/s164/).
