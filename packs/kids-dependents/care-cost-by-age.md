# Care cost by age

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-07

Childcare cost and 529 contribution timing follow the spending and obligations pack. Full-time care changes at school entry and after-school care ends at the dependent care age limit. 529 contributions stop at college start. Use the spending pack's childcare-by-age and education-saving-timeline entries for those rules. This entry does not restate the ages or calculate a household amount.

Sources: [IRS Publication 503: child and dependent care](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p503).
