# Apply 2026 child tax credit limits and identification rules

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-05

For 2026, the maximum child tax credit is $2,200 per qualifying child, and the maximum potentially refundable portion is $1,700. These are ceilings, not an automatic payment. Refundability depends on the applicable earned-income calculation, tax liability and other restrictions. Use the deterministic return calculation rather than multiplying household dependent count by the headline amount.

A qualifying child generally must be under age 17 at year-end and satisfy the relationship, residence, support and dependent tests. Valid employment-authorized Social Security numbers and their issuance deadlines matter for both the qualifying child and the applicable taxpayer or spouse. An identification number sufficient for another purpose does not necessarily qualify for this credit.

The full-credit income threshold is $400,000 on a joint return and $200,000 for other filing statuses, with phaseout above those levels. Recheck projected income after bonuses, business distributions and Roth conversions. Recommendation: distinguish an established dependent from an eligible credit claimant and review the current Schedule 8812 instructions when preparing the return.

Sources: [IRS Revenue Procedure 2025-32: 2026 child credit amounts](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-25-32.pdf), [IRS Child Tax Credit eligibility](https://www.irs.gov/credits-deductions/individuals/child-tax-credit).
