# Check dependent qualification for a young child

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-05

A toddler generally satisfies the age portion of the qualifying-child rules, but age alone is insufficient. Review the relationship, residence, support and joint-return tests along with the general dependent restrictions. For a qualifying child, the support question is normally whether the child provided more than half of their own support. It is not the qualifying-relative rule requiring the taxpayer personally to provide more than half.

A child generally must live with the taxpayer for more than half the year, with exceptions for temporary absences and other specified situations. Keep residence and support evidence, especially when parents live apart or another relative could claim the child. One child cannot simply be assigned to multiple returns because several adults contributed to care.

Recommendation: establish dependent qualification before counting any tax benefit. The child tax credit, dependent care credit and employer benefits each add separate requirements and age tests. A child's dependent status does not guarantee every credit, and an income phaseout can eliminate a credit without changing whether the child is a dependent.

Sources: [IRS Publication 501: enduring dependent and custody rules](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p501), [IRS Child Tax Credit eligibility](https://www.irs.gov/credits-deductions/individuals/child-tax-credit).
