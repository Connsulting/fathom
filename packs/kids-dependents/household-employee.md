# Recognize household employer duties for a nanny

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-05

A caregiver working in the household can be an employee when the household controls both what work is done and how it is done. Calling the person a contractor or hiring through a list does not determine classification. A caregiver operating an independent business or caring for children in their own home may have different treatment.

For 2026, paying one household employee $3,000 or more in cash wages generally triggers Social Security and Medicare taxes on covered wages, subject to the publication's family and age exceptions. Separate federal unemployment rules can apply, and state employment obligations must also be checked. Qualifying care expenses and employer tax compliance are separate questions.

Recommendation: establish payroll records and the required employer identification before year-end forms are due. Publication 926 sets February 1, 2027 for furnishing and filing applicable 2026 Forms W-2, and April 15, 2027 for Schedule H with the income tax return, subject to extensions where allowed. Budget employer taxes with the care cost and retain payment records for dependent care substantiation.

Sources: [IRS Publication 926 (2026): household employers](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p926), [IRS Publication 503: enduring qualifying-care rules](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p503).
