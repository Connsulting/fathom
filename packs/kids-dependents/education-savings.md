# Coordinate education savings with household priorities

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-05

Education savings should be tied to a beneficiary, a funding goal and qualifying uses. A young child's long horizon does not by itself establish the right contribution amount or investment selection. Keep account ownership, contribution sources and intended expenses distinct from the household's emergency reserves and retirement decisions.

Virginia's qualifying education-account deduction generally belongs to the plan-record owner and is ordinarily limited to $4,000 per account per year, with carryforward and an age-70 exception. Use the virginia-resident pack before assuming that a relative's deposit produces that relative's deduction. The current program description also distinguishes beneficiary-wide withdrawal limits from the state's account-based contribution deduction.

Recommendation: retain receipts and payment dates when withdrawals eventually begin and coordinate education tax benefits so the same expense is not used twice. A beneficiary change or leftover balance requires reviewing the specific family-transfer and withdrawal rules. Federal permission for a 529-to-Roth IRA rollover does not establish Virginia recapture treatment; the current program description flags that state question as unresolved.

Sources: [Code of Virginia 58.1-322.03: Virginia529 deductions](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title58.1/section58.1-322.03/), [Invest529 Program Description, October 1, 2026](https://www.invest529.com/uploads/files/va529_invest_program_description.pdf).
