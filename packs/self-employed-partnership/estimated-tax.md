# Estimated tax for uneven business income

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-05

## Rule

The general individual federal estimated tax rule applies when expected tax due after withholding and credits is at least $1,000 and withholding and credits fall below the required annual payment. That payment generally compares 90% of current-year tax with 100% of prior-year tax. Use 110% of prior-year tax when prior-year AGI exceeds $150,000, or $75,000 for married filing separately. The prior return must cover a full year. Special rules can change this comparison. [IRS Publication 505, 2026 estimated tax](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p505)

## 2026 deadlines

For calendar-year individuals, ordinary installments fall on April 15, June 15 and September 15, 2026, and January 15, 2027. These are not evenly spaced calendar quarters. The underpayment calculation considers each payment period; a large late payment does not automatically cure an earlier shortfall. [IRS Publication 505, 2026 estimated tax](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p505)

## Trap and interaction

The annualized income installment method may better match irregular receipts, but it requires the supporting calculation and Form 2210 with the return. Coordinate business income, partner payments, spouse withholding and conversions in a household projection. A safe harbor can address underpayment penalties while still leaving a balance due with the return. This pack does not calculate a household payment. [IRS Publication 505, 2026 estimated tax](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p505)
