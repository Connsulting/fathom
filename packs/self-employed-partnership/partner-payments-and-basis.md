# Partner income, guaranteed payments and basis

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-05

## Rule

A partnership generally reports income on Form 1065 and allocates items through Schedule K-1. Partners are not employees for distributions or guaranteed payments and should not receive a W-2 in place of a K-1 for those amounts. Guaranteed payments are determined without regard to partnership income and generally are not subject to income tax withholding. [IRS Publication 541, partnerships](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p541) [IRS paying yourself](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/paying-yourself)

## Review

A partner generally reports the distributive share whether or not cash was distributed. General partner self-employment income ordinarily includes the business share and qualifying guaranteed payments. The limited partner exception is a specific tax rule, not something established solely by calling an LLC owner passive or giving that owner a limited title. Guaranteed payments for services can remain subject to self-employment tax. [IRS Publication 541, partnerships](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p541) [IRS business entity tax treatment](https://www.irs.gov/faqs/small-business-self-employed-other-business/entities)

## Trap and interaction

A cash distribution is not automatically an additional deduction or tax-free profit. Track outside basis, liability changes, losses and distributions, and apply basis, at-risk and passive limitations where relevant. Guaranteed payments also interact with retirement compensation and the QBI exclusion for service payments. [IRS Publication 541, partnerships](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p541) [IRS calculation of plan compensation for partnerships](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/calculation-of-plan-compensation-for-partnerships) [IRS qualified business income deduction](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/qualified-business-income-deduction)
