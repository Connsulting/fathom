# Spouse ownership, employment and qualified joint ventures

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-05

## Rule

A business jointly owned by spouses is generally a partnership unless a qualifying exception applies. A qualified joint venture generally requires a joint individual return, both spouses materially participating, both electing the treatment and a business not held in the name of a state-law partnership or LLC. Each spouse reports the allocated business activity separately under that election. [IRS married couples in business](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/married-couples-in-business) [IRS Publication 541, partnerships](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p541)

## Review

Distinguish a co-owner from a spouse who works as an employee. Employment treatment has payroll requirements and creates a different compensation record from a partner's K-1. Community-property ownership has separate federal treatment rules; do not apply that exception solely because the business is owned by spouses. [IRS married couples in business](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/married-couples-in-business) [IRS Publication 3402, LLC classification](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p3402)

## Trap and interaction

An LLC with two spouse members does not ordinarily become a sole proprietorship merely by filing one Schedule C. Confirm ownership and classification before changing returns or retirement plan sponsorship. If corporate tax treatment applies, review the S corporation spouse attribution rules before assuming ordinary employee benefits are available. Spousal IRA eligibility is separate and depends on the IRA rules and joint compensation. [IRS married couples in business](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/married-couples-in-business) [IRS Publication 3402, LLC classification](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p3402) [IRS Publication 15-B, 2026 fringe benefits](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15b)
