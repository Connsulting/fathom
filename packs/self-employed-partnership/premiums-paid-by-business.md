# Premiums paid by the business

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-07

Premiums paid by the business are outside the household ledger. They do not appear in household healthcare spending. The spending and obligations pack states that boundary. Deducting self-employed health insurance on the return does not put the premium into the household spending classes.

Sources: [IRS Form 7206 instructions, self-employed health insurance](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i7206).
