# Plan aggregation and employee eligibility

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-05

## Individual and employer limits

The employee elective deferral limit generally follows the person across applicable 401(k), 403(b), SIMPLE and SARSEP participation, with plan-specific limits and catch-up rules. Changing jobs or adding a Solo 401(k) does not reset that individual limit. A 457(b) plan has a separate deferral limit, so do not apply a blanket aggregation rule to every plan type. [IRS deferral limits across more than one plan](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/how-much-salary-can-you-defer-if-youre-eligible-for-more-than-one-retirement-plan)

Employer annual additions limits follow different rules. Plans of the same employer and relevant related employers must be reviewed together; a separately named business does not automatically create another limit. Controlled group rules can treat related employers as one employer. Special 403(b) aggregation rules can also matter for a participant who controls another business. [IRS Publication 560, small business retirement plans](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p560) [IRS Publication 571, 403(b) aggregation](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p571) [IRS explanation of Section 415 plan aggregation](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/issue-snapshot-403b-plan-application-of-irc-section-415c-when-a-403b-plan-is-aggregated-with-a-section-401a-defined-contribution-plan)

## Employee coverage

A Solo 401(k)'s lack of ordinary employee nondiscrimination testing relies on having no eligible common-law employees. Hiring employees can end that treatment. For 401(k) plan years beginning after 2024, long-term part-time eligibility generally considers 500 hours in each of 2 consecutive 12-month periods, subject to the applicable age and other rules. A SEP has its own employee eligibility and contribution requirements. [IRS one-participant 401(k) plans](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/one-participant-401k-plans) [IRS Publication 560, small business retirement plans](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p560) [IRS retirement plan FAQs regarding SEPs](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/retirement-plans-faqs-regarding-seps)

## Trap and interaction

Review related businesses, spouse employment and employee service records before treating a plan as owner-only. Track outside deferrals and reconcile employer contributions before calculating 2026 capacity. Entity changes in the business packs require a fresh sponsorship and coverage review.
