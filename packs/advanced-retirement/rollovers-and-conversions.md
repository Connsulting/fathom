# Rollovers, Roth conversions and income timing

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-05

## Rule

A pretax retirement rollover and a Roth conversion have different income tax results. Eligible pretax amounts rolled into a traditional account generally preserve deferral; pretax amounts converted to Roth generally produce income in the conversion year. A Roth conversion cannot be recharacterized back to a traditional IRA. Income recognized on conversion can change estimated tax and income-based deductions or credits. [IRS Publication 590-A, IRA contributions and conversions](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p590a) [IRS Publication 505, 2026 estimated tax](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p505)

## Review

Separate a trustee transfer from a distribution paid to the individual. A paid distribution can have a 60-day rollover deadline and employer-plan withholding rules, while direct rollovers follow different withholding treatment. The IRA one-rollover-per-year restriction generally does not apply to trustee-to-trustee transfers or Roth conversions. Required minimum distributions cannot be rolled over. [IRS Publication 590-A, IRA contributions and conversions](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p590a) [IRS Topic 413, retirement plan rollovers](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc413)

## Trap and interaction

An employer plan is not required to accept every eligible rollover, and inherited accounts and SIMPLE arrangements can have additional restrictions. A pretax employer-plan rollover into a traditional IRA can change the year-end balance used in a backdoor Roth pro-rata calculation. Preserve Form 1099-R, Form 5498 and basis records and review the actual transaction year rather than treating conversion reporting as a prior-year IRA contribution. This content is advisory and does not initiate a rollover or conversion. [IRS Publication 590-A, IRA contributions and conversions](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p590a) [IRS Form 8606 instructions, IRA basis and conversions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8606) [IRS Forms 1099-R and 5498 instructions for 2026](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1099r)
