# 2026 contribution limits and separate tax buckets

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-05

## 2026 limits

The ordinary 401(k) elective deferral limit is $24,500. If catch-ups are available, the general age-50-or-older catch-up is $8,000; for someone who attains age 60, 61, 62 or 63 during 2026, the higher catch-up is $11,250 instead. The defined contribution annual additions limit is $72,000 before eligible catch-up contributions. Relevant employer contribution compensation is capped at $360,000. Compensation and deduction limits can reduce what a participant may contribute. [IRS Notice 2025-67, 2026 retirement limits](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/n-25-67.pdf) [IRS Publication 560, small business retirement plans](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p560)

## Distinguish the buckets

Pretax and designated Roth deferrals share the employee elective deferral limit. Employer contributions, employee after-tax contributions and allocated forfeitures also use annual additions capacity. A designated Roth deferral is different from a voluntary after-tax non-Roth contribution. The ordinary IRA contribution limit is $7,500, with a $1,100 catch-up at age 50 or older; this IRA limit is separate from workplace plan deferrals. [IRS Notice 2025-67, 2026 retirement limits](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/n-25-67.pdf) [IRS retirement topics, contribution types](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/plan-participant-employee/retirement-topics-contributions)

## Trap and interaction

A new account does not create another individual deferral or IRA limit. These are 2026 statutory figures, not a contribution recommendation or a projection of a later year's limits. Use the aggregation, deadline and catch-up entries before computing remaining capacity. [IRS deferral limits across more than one plan](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/how-much-salary-can-you-defer-if-youre-eligible-for-more-than-one-retirement-plan) [IRS Publication 590-A, IRA contributions and conversions](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p590a)
