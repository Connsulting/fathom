# Review the employer benefits inventory

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-05

Treat each employer's benefit menu as a set of separate tax rules. Gather the current plan descriptions, payroll elections, employer contributions, eligibility dates and claim deadlines before comparing choices. A cafeteria plan can let an employee choose qualified benefits instead of taxable cash, but exclusions depend on the benefit and the written arrangement. A benefit's availability does not establish the household's eligibility for its tax treatment.

Recommendation: compare the owner and spouse together. Record which health plan covers each person, whether either health FSA can reimburse the other's expenses, and whether both employers offer dependent care assistance. Coordinate retirement deferrals across job changes and any self-employed plan. Employer plan limits can be lower than the statutory ceiling.

A birth, leave period, job change or business ownership change should trigger a fresh inventory. Ask the plan administrator which election changes the actual plan permits and when requests must arrive. Recalculate withholding when taxable compensation or pretax deductions change; the benefit selection alone does not update the household's overall tax projection.

Sources: [IRS Publication 15-B (2026)](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15b), [IRS Publication 505 (2026)](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p505).
