# Reconcile payroll and taxable benefits

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-05

A fringe benefit is taxable unless a specific exclusion applies. The exclusion may differ for federal income tax, Social Security and Medicare, so benefit descriptions and gross pay alone do not establish the correct taxable wage amount. Reconcile the final payroll statement with Form W-2 and the benefit account records before preparing the return.

Dependent care assistance appears in Form W-2 box 10, including amounts that may become taxable after the household's eligibility calculation. Employer HSA contributions, including qualifying cafeteria-plan salary reductions, need to be reconciled with total HSA contributions and Form 8889. Do not claim a second personal deduction for amounts already excluded through payroll.

Business ownership changes can alter fringe-benefit treatment. More-than-2% S corporation shareholders and partners do not receive every ordinary employee cafeteria-plan exclusion. If either status applies, route the benefit to the s-corp-owner or self-employed-partnership pack before assuming the W-2 arrangement carries over unchanged. Recommendation: request corrections from payroll when a form conflicts with the underlying benefit records.

Sources: [IRS Publication 15-B (2026)](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15b), [IRS Publication 969: enduring HSA and FSA rules](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p969).
