# Premiums through payroll

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-07

Health premiums paid through payroll are outside the household ledger. They do not appear as household healthcare spending. The spending and obligations pack states the same boundary. A household medical expense that the owner pays directly is a different amount.

Sources: [IRS Publication 15-B (2026)](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15b).
