# Coordinate employer retirement deferrals

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-05

For 2026, the employee elective-deferral limit for a typical 401(k) or 403(b) is $24,500. The general age-50 catch-up is $8,000; participants attaining ages 60 through 63 during 2026 instead have an $11,250 catch-up where the plan permits it. These are individual limits, and pretax and designated Roth elective deferrals share the applicable ceiling.

Combine deferrals across former and current employers and a side business's Solo 401(k). Employer contributions are governed by separate limits, so a match does not simply consume the employee elective-deferral limit. A governmental 457(b) has a separate limitation and should not be combined mechanically with the 401(k) bucket.

Recommendation: reconcile year-to-date payroll totals before a job change or final payroll election. Match formulas, vesting and payroll cutoff dates come from the actual plan. Send retirement-plan details to the advanced-retirement and self-employed packs when business income also exists. Do not assume opening another account creates a second employee-deferral allowance.

Sources: [IRS Notice 2025-67: 2026 retirement limits](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/n-25-67.pdf), [IRS contributions across multiple retirement plans](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/how-much-salary-can-you-defer-if-youre-eligible-for-more-than-one-retirement-plan).
