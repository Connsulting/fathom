# Size and coordinate the health FSA

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-05

For plan years beginning in 2026, employee salary reductions into a health FSA cannot exceed $3,400. If the plan permits a carryover, the 2026 maximum carryover is $680. The employer can impose a lower limit, and a carryover is optional. A plan may instead offer a grace period under the applicable rules; confirm the plan's provision rather than assuming unused funds survive.

Recommendation: base the election on eligible expenses and reimbursements expected during the coverage period. Distinguish the deadline for incurring an expense from the later deadline for submitting a claim. Save invoices and proof that insurance or another account did not reimburse the same expense.

A general-purpose health FSA can make a covered person ineligible to contribute to an HSA, including where coverage comes through the spouse. Check limited-purpose alternatives and the effect of prior-year carryover or grace-period coverage before switching to an HSA arrangement. The health FSA and dependent care FSA have different limits, eligible expenses and forfeiture rules.

Sources: [IRS Publication 15-B (2026)](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15b), [IRS Revenue Procedure 2025-32: 2026 adjustments](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-25-32.pdf), [IRS Publication 969: enduring HSA and FSA rules](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p969).
