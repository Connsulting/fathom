# Coordinate dependent care benefits across employers

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-05

For 2026, the dependent care assistance exclusion is up to $7,500, or $3,750 for married filing separately. This is a household tax exclusion limit, not a fresh allowance at each employer. Include the owner and spouse's dependent care FSAs and other employer-provided dependent care benefits in the reconciliation. Earned-income and qualifying-care restrictions can reduce the excludable amount below the headline ceiling.

Expenses generally must allow the taxpayer and spouse, if filing jointly, to work or look for work and must concern a qualifying person. A child under age 13 when care is provided can qualify. Check the actual employer plan for available elections, reimbursement procedures and deadlines; the statutory increase does not require every plan to offer the full amount.

Do not use the same expenses for both an exclusion and the dependent care credit. For 2026, the credit's expense ceiling remains $3,000 for one qualifying person or $6,000 for two or more, while the credit rate now ranges from 20% to 50%. Use the kids-dependents pack for the coordinated comparison.

Sources: [IRS Publication 15-B (2026)](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15b), [IRS child and dependent care credit: 2026 rules](https://www.irs.gov/credits-deductions/individuals/child-and-dependent-care-credit-information).
