# Use payroll withholding in the household tax plan

Tax year: 2026

Reviewed: 2026-11-05

Wage withholding should reflect the household return, including the spouse's wages, business income, investment income and changing credits. Selecting married filing jointly on both Forms W-4 without coordinating the multiple-job rules can produce too little withholding. Recheck the projection after a bonus, leave period, birth or change in pretax benefit elections.

Publication 505's general estimated-tax framework compares payments with current-year and prior-year tax. Higher-income taxpayers can face a larger prior-year safe-harbor percentage. A safe harbor concerns underpayment penalties and does not cap the tax ultimately due. Do not choose an extra withholding amount solely from last year's refund.

Recommendation: reconcile actual year-to-date withholding and remaining pay dates with the deterministic tax projection before submitting a revised Form W-4. Federal withholding is generally allocated across payment periods under the applicable rules, which can make a late-year payroll adjustment useful. State withholding requires its own calculation and instructions. A revised election needs an actual payroll confirmation before the projection treats the increase as paid.

Sources: [IRS Publication 505 (2026)](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p505).
